About Us

Our Story

Creating Extraordinary Events Since 2009

Founded in 2009 in Chennai City by Danial, MESSIAH Events & Decor. began as a boutique wedding planning service with a single mission: to transform clients' visions into extraordinary realities. Over the past 15 years, we've grown into a full-service event management company, expanding our capabilities across corporate events, concerts, private celebrations, and everything in between.

Today, our team of 30+ certified event professionals has orchestrated over 500 successful events — from intimate 10-person board dinners to 5,000-person music festivals. Our commitment to creativity, precision, and personalized service has earned us recognition as Chennai most trusted event management firm.

Our Mission

To create extraordinary experiences that connect people, celebrate milestones, and leave lasting impressions — delivered with professionalism, creativity, and heart.

Our Strengths

Why Choose MESSIAH Events & Decor.?

We bring expertise, creativity, and dedication to every event we manage, ensuring flawless experiences every single time.

Professional Planners

Our certified event planning team brings 15+ years of combined expertise to every project. Each planner is trained in the latest industry standards and best practices. We assign a dedicated planner to your event who manages everything from concept to close.

Creative Concepts

We design bespoke themes that reflect your personality and vision, not cookie-cutter templates. Our in-house creative team collaborates on decor, lighting, entertainment, and atmosphere. Every event we produce is a unique, Instagram-worthy experience your guests won't forget.

Budget-Friendly Packages

We believe extraordinary events shouldn't break the bank. Our team works with your budget to maximize quality and impact, negotiating the best rates with our trusted vendor network. We offer transparent pricing with no hidden fees.

Vendor Coordination

Managing vendors can be overwhelming — we handle it all so you don't have to. We work with a vetted network of 200+ caterers, photographers, florists, and entertainers. Our strong vendor relationships often result in exclusive pricing and priority service for our clients.

On-Time Execution

We plan every event with a detailed timeline and run-of-show document. Our on-site team conducts rehearsals and maintains minute-by-minute coordination on the event day. In 15 years, we have never missed a single critical deadline.

Personalized Support

You get a dedicated event planner as your single point of contact from day one. We offer unlimited consultations, real-time progress updates, and 24/7 support in the final 48 hours before your event. You're never alone in the planning process.
